FAQ

Things people ask us.

01 How does a project start?
+
Everything begins with a 20-minute discovery call — no pitches, no presentations. We talk through your goals, audience, and what you're building. From there, we propose the right package and get started within a week.
02 Do you work with international clients?
+
Yes — our Remote-First package is built exactly for that. You shoot your footage, we handle everything else: scripts, story, editing, colour and delivery. Timezone-friendly async workflows, tight turnarounds.
03 What does turnaround look like?
+
Typical turnaround for edited reels is 3–5 business days from approved script. Full production projects are scoped during onboarding. We always communicate timelines upfront — no surprises.
04 What platforms do you optimise for?
+
Primarily Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, but we also deliver for LinkedIn, TikTok, and long-form YouTube. Every format has its own pacing, hook structure, and caption strategy — we build for the platform, not just the brief.

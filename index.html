FAQ

Things founders ask us.

01 How does a founder project start?
+
Everything begins with a 20-minute discovery call — no pitches, no presentations. We talk through your startup, your audience, and how comfortable you are on camera. From there, we build a content package around your reality and get started within a week.
02 I'm camera-shy — can you still make this work?
+
Yes — most founders start this way. We script for your natural speaking style, keep early reels short and low-pressure, and build confidence over the first few shoots. Most founders relax into it within their first two or three reels.
03 Do you work with international founders?
+
Yes — our Remote-First package is built exactly for that. You shoot your own footage or b-roll, we handle everything else: scripts, story, editing, motion graphics and delivery. Timezone-friendly async workflows, tight turnarounds.
04 What does turnaround look like?
+
Typical turnaround for edited founder reels is 3–5 business days from approved script. Launch-day or "introducing the next big thing" reels are scoped during onboarding. We always communicate timelines upfront — no surprises.
05 Do you work with pre-launch / stealth startups?
+
Absolutely. Pre-launch founder branding is one of our favourite things to build — teaser reels, "building in public" formats, and founder-story content that primes an audience before you ever announce the product.
